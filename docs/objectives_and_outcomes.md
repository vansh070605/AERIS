# 💧 AERIS Project: Objectives & Outcomes

## 🚀 Mission Overview
The **AI-Enhanced Risk Intelligence System (AERIS)** is a high-precision, proactive emergency management platform designed specifically for Bihar's annual flood crisis. Its core mission is to transform flood response from reactive chaos into AI-driven, proactive precision.

---

## 🏗️ Modules: Objectives and Outcomes

### 1. Live Risk Map (Forensic GIS)
- **Objective**: Provide a high-precision GIS interface for real-time situational awareness and spatial risk assessment.
- **Outcome**: 
    - Interactive district-level heatmaps based on predictive risk metrics.
    - Real-time counters for **Population at Risk** and **Active Incidents**.
    - Context-aware "Situation Room" telemetry logs for live satellite and sensor data.

### 2. 7-Day Forecast Simulator
- **Objective**: Empower researchers and planners with a "What-If" tool to project flood impacts 168 hours in advance.
- **Outcome**: 
    - A multi-variate simulation engine using **XGBoost** to forecast flood probabilities.
    - Dynamic time-series visualizations showing predictive trends for the week ahead.
    - Ability to simulate different rainfall scenarios to prepare for extreme events (100-year floods).

### 3. Real-Time River Pulse
- **Objective**: Implement a live telemetry network to monitor Bihar's 5 major river basins with zero-latency ML analysis.
- **Outcome**: 
    - Automated monitoring of **18 critical gauge stations** (including CWC and local barrage data).
    - Real-time **Breach Probability** scores for aging embankments.
    - Visual alerts (Normal, Warning, Danger) with trend indicators (Rising/Falling/Steady).

### 4. Resilience Scorecard
- **Objective**: Quantify and rank district preparedness to identify systemic vulnerabilities before flooding begins.
- **Outcome**: 
    - A multi-dimensional scoring system evaluating **Infrastructure, Medical, and Shelter** readiness.
    - A prioritized "District Ranking" table to guide NGO and Government resource pre-positioning.
    - Forensic accountability for embankment maintenance and resource allocation.

### 5. Post-Event Learning (Forensics)
- **Objective**: Reconstruct the timeline of hydraulic stress to refine future ML models and learn from historical breaches.
- **Outcome**: 
    - A forensic "Retrospective" interface showing how water levels surged during previous disasters.
    - Detailed breach analysis logs to improve the **Breach XGBoost Model** for subsequent years.

### 6. ML Predictor (Inference Probe)
- **Objective**: Facilitate rapid hypothesis testing through manual parameter override of the core AI models.
- **Outcome**: 
    - User-friendly sliders for rainfall intensity, embankment age, and maintenance scores.
    - Instantaneous classification of breach risk (Low, Medium, High).
    - Transparent metadata showing the logical profile used for each prediction.

### 7. Scholarly Research Review
- **Objective**: Position AERIS within the academic and professional landscape of disaster management.
- **Outcome**: 
    - A comprehensive research-style overview articulating the "Forensic Gap" in current systems (CWC, NDMA).
    - A technical critique of text-based bulletins vs. AERIS's spatial ML triggers.

---

## 🏆 Final Impact Goals
- **7-Day Evacuation Lead Time**: Saving lives through early, localized warnings.
- **Resource Efficiency**: Directing aid based on statistical vulnerability rather than uniform distribution.
- **Infrastructure Longevity**: Prioritizing embankment repairs using predictive breach logic.
